HACKATHON: BharatCode AI Hackathon 2026

PROBLEM:
Bharat ke 65% log gaon me rehte hai. Kisan ko mandi price nahi pata,
student ko Hindi me simple samajh nahi aata, aur health ki basic jaankari
ke liye doctor ke paas jaana padta hai. English AI apps unke liye mushkil hai.

SOLUTION:
Bharat Saathi AI - Ek simple website jo Hindi + English me baat karti hai.
3 Modules:
1. Kisan Saathi - Mandi Price, Mausam, Fasal Advice
2. Student Saathi - NCERT Help, Exam Tips
3. Health Saathi - Fever, Diet, Yoga Info

No Login. No App Download. Kisi bhi mobile me khulta hai.

TECH USED:
HTML, CSS, JavaScript - Pure Frontend, No API Key Needed

IMPACT:
- Kisan ko sahi mandi price milega = Zyada paisa
- Student ko Hindi me concept samajh aayega = Better marks
- Health ki basic jaankari = Kam dar

FUTURE PLAN:
- Voice Support in Bhojpuri / Hindi
- Real Live Mandi API + Weather API
- WhatsApp Bot Version
